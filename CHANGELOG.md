# Changelog

All notable changes to Operator Skills.

Versions are `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`:

- **MAJOR** — a skill is removed or renamed, or output filenames change. Something you
  relied on may stop working.
- **MINOR** — a skill is added, or an existing one gains behaviour. Nothing breaks.
- **PATCH** — wording, fixes and description tuning so skills fire more reliably.

---

## v1.0.0 — unreleased

First release. Twenty-two skills.

**The keystone**

- `business-brief` — interviews you once and writes the file every other skill reads.
  Records what is genuinely unknown rather than guessing, and distinguishes a price you
  chose not to give from one nobody has asked you for yet.

**Added — marketing & campaigns**

- `launch-campaign` — dated launch plan sequenced backwards, with blockers. Checks whether
  you have the capacity to serve the demand before planning to create it.
- `ad-creative-lab` — paid ad variants where exactly one thing changes, so a win tells you
  something.
- `market-research` — labels every claim as evidence, inference or unknown, and will say
  when a question cannot be answered.

**Added — content & social**

- `content-calendar` — dated ideas each with a reason to exist; leaves slots empty rather
  than filling them with "share a tip".
- `social-post-writer` — a separate post per channel, never one pasted everywhere.
- `video-script` — hook-first, with a read time counted honestly.

**Added — sales & revenue**

- `sales-script` — one offer, one real price, and the price said plainly.
- `objection-handler` — honest answers, including when the objection is correct and you
  should agree. Refuses manipulative framing.
- `proposal-writer` — scope, exclusions and price.

**Added — operations & systems**

- `sop-writer` — one job, with failure modes and a review date.
- `role-scorecard` — outcomes with numbers, not a list of duties.
- `workflow-designer` — stages, handoffs, and an honest mark on what needs a human.

**Added — finance & strategy**

- `financial-summary` — drafts are not owed, refunds come off revenue, undated invoices are
  never late.
- `pricing-strategy` — a price with the reasoning shown.

**Added — HR & team**

- `job-description` — a role brief and an advert that agree with each other.
- `onboarding-plan` — a first-90-days plan with a real day-30 review.

**Added — customer success**

- `enquiry-reply` — the only skill that can send. Shows the exact message and recipient and
  asks first, one message at a time. Quotes a rate rather than a total when the enquirer has
  not said how many staff, payslips or units — an inferred count becomes a quoted price, and
  a quoted price is a promise.
- `client-report` — outcomes rather than activity, honest about quiet months.

**Added — website & copy**

- `website-copy` — page by page against a real sitemap.
- `landing-page` — one offer, one action.
- `email-sequence` — each email has a job; real personalisation marked rather than faked.
