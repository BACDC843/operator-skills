# Operator Skills

Twenty-two skills for running a small business, built for Claude.

One of them interviews you once and writes down what your business sells, to whom, at what
price and in what voice. The other twenty-one read that file. You stop explaining your
business at the start of every conversation.

## Start here

Install **`business-brief`** first and run it. It asks about a dozen questions, takes about
fifteen minutes, and writes a `business-brief.md` file.

Everything else works better once that file exists — and several skills are close to
useless without it, because they need your real prices.

## One skill can send. The other twenty-one cannot

`enquiry-reply` is the only skill here that can do anything outside your own files, and it
shows you the exact message and the exact address and asks first, every time, one message
at a time.

Everything else writes a file and hands it to you. Nothing posts, publishes, books or emails
on your behalf.

→ **[Which skills act and which only draft](docs/what-acts-and-what-drafts.md)**

## What these skills will not do

Worth knowing before you buy rather than after:

- **They will not invent a fact.** Not a price, a client name, an award or a statistic.
  Where something is missing you get a marked gap instead of a confident guess.
- **They will not give tax, legal or employment advice.** Where a question is regulated the
  skill says so and tells you who to ask.
- **They will not write false urgency.** No invented deadlines, countdowns or slot counts.
- **They are not an app.** They are text files you install into your own Claude account.
  There is no login, no dashboard and nothing to cancel.
